How it goes

      
      four steps, no surprises
    


    

      

        01
        
Listen

        
You send us a link and an honest paragraph about what's wrong. We take the site apart
        on our bench and write back with what we found — free, whether you hire us or not.

      

      

        02
        
Draft

        
We build a working prototype you can click through — not a slide deck, not a mockup.
        Real code, wrong colors, right ideas.

      

      

        03
        
Rewrite

        
Then we throw the first version away and do it again, better. Every time.
        It is, literally, the name of the company.

      

      

        04
        
Hand over

        
You own all of it — files, domain, a plain-English document on how it works.
        We stay one email away. Forever.
